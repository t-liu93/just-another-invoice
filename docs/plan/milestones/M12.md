## Goal and Scope

**Goal**: extend the existing invoice lifecycle into an auditable document family that supports ordinary invoices, formal staged advance/final invoicing, corrections, refunds and exact output retention without changing historical issued documents or double-counting cash, VAT or revenue.

Included:

- `STANDARD`, `ADVANCE`, `FINAL` and `CREDIT_NOTE` formal document kinds.
- An explicit, immutable billing mode per accepted quote: direct invoice, M11.5 receipt-only deposits, or formal advance/final invoicing.
- One or more formal Advance Invoices followed by at most one Final Invoice for a formal quote chain.
- Gross-amount and percentage Advance input, with deterministic backend allocation from the accepted Quote snapshots.
- Full and controlled partial Credit Notes against any issued Standard, Advance or Final Invoice.
- Actual Refund ledger entries linked to Credit Notes, plus Refund Confirmation PDF/email.
- Guided direct/advance replacement, compensating invoices for an erroneous Credit Note, and whole-formal-project cancellation draft generation.
- Independent lifecycle, settlement and credit states; project/document-chain totals; append-only chain lifecycle events.
- Exactly-once BTW/ICP/P&L/Dashboard effects, structured correction-period warnings, issue-time party snapshots and retained exact PDF artifacts.
- One unified invoice list with kind filters/badges and a complete Quote/document-chain timeline.

Excluded and explicitly deferred:

- Standalone Credit Notes, unallocated customer balances and customer credit wallets.
- Refunding an unconverted M11.5 Quote deposit, or moving/reapplying a receipt-only payment to another document.
- Overpayments, negative user-entered payments and bank-feed reconciliation.
- Multi-currency/FX and formal cross-border Advance/Final invoicing.
- Selected-line or milestone-based Advance input and more than one open Advance DRAFT per Quote.
- A fifth `DEBIT_NOTE` kind or crediting a Credit Note.
- App-managed VAT-return filing state, filing-period locks or automated suppletie submission.
- Dutch UI/PDF locale; M12 ships English and Chinese only and re-verifies Dutch-facing terminology before a future NL locale.

## Frozen Product and Architecture Decisions

- [x] **D1 · Quote-selected mode**: no Customer classification is added. A Quote begins `UNSET`; the first relevant action atomically locks it to `DIRECT_INVOICE`, `RECEIPT_ONLY` or `FORMAL_ADVANCE`. Deleting later drafts or payments never unlocks it.
- [x] **D2 · No mode mixing**: direct conversion, receipt-only deposits and formal Advance/Final documents cannot coexist on one Quote. Existing M11.5 behaviour remains the `RECEIPT_ONLY` branch.
- [x] **D3 · Formal VAT boundary**: new formal Advance/Final chains support only `NL_DOMESTIC`; unsupported treatments return explicit `422`. Credit Notes inherit and can correct every VAT treatment already supported by their source, including ICP effects.
- [x] **D4 · Advance input**: the user supplies either a gross amount or a percentage. A percentage is always of the original accepted Quote gross total; the backend alone allocates taxable/VAT buckets. Selected-line/milestone Advances are out.
- [x] **D5 · One Advance draft**: a formal Quote has at most one open Advance DRAFT. Issuing or deleting it permits the next draft; deletion does not change the Quote mode.
- [x] **D6 · Final without payment blocking**: unpaid Advances do not block Final creation or issue. Payments remain attached to their original charge documents.
- [x] **D7 · Guarded Final editing**: a Final DRAFT may increase or decrease lines, quantities, prices, discounts and rates, but Quote, Customer, currency and `NL_DOMESTIC` treatment are fixed; its date cannot precede an applied Advance; every applied VAT bucket must remain covered; the UI shows Quote→Final variance.
- [x] **D8 · Final freeze**: while a Final DRAFT exists, new Advance issue, Advance Credit Note and Advance replacement are blocked. Deleting the Final DRAFT removes that freeze. Only one Final DRAFT/issued Final may exist.
- [x] **D9 · Automatic immutable applications**: Final creation applies every issued Advance's then-remaining net charge in stable order. The application snapshot never changes; later Advance credits are independent correction events.
- [x] **D10 · Source-bound corrections**: Standard, Advance and Final documents can each be credited independently at any stage after issue. A Credit Note reverses only the source document's actual remaining charge/tax basis; it never rewrites the source or Final application history.
- [x] **D11 · Partial Credit input**: the user chooses full remaining coverage or selected source lines. Each selected line uses either quantity or a fixed gross amount; the backend derives all net/VAT values from immutable remaining source snapshots.
- [x] **D12 · No credit-of-credit**: a Credit Note cannot be credited. An erroneous issued Credit Note gets a linked compensating invoice DRAFT: `ADVANCE` only when safely pre-Final, otherwise a supplemental `STANDARD` invoice.
- [x] **D13 · Separate Credit sequence**: Standard/Advance/Final share the existing invoice sequence. Credit Notes use a separate typed, configurable, concurrency-safe sequential series.
- [x] **D14 · Refund ledger**: a refund is a positive `Payment` amount with `direction=REFUND`, linked to an issued Credit Note. There are no standalone refunds, negative payment input or unallocated credit.
- [x] **D15 · Mutable cash with chain revalidation**: incoming payments and refunds remain editable/deletable, but every mutation locks and revalidates the full affected chain; any mutation that causes over-refund, invalid coverage or inconsistent settlement is rejected atomically.
- [x] **D16 · Independent statuses**: keep lifecycle `DRAFT/SENT/COMPLETED/CANCELLED`; add orthogonal settlement and credit states. `paid_status` gains `NOT_APPLICABLE`; `due_amount` and `refund_due_amount` remain separate.
- [x] **D17 · Revenue timing**: Advance issue does not create P/L revenue. Final issue recognises the full then-current project net; Standard issue is unchanged. Standard/Final Credit Notes reverse revenue on credit date. An Advance Credit Note reverses revenue only when a Final was already issued; `affects_revenue` is frozen at credit issue.
- [x] **D18 · Corrections are dated events**: BTW/ICP consume the issued Credit Note on its own date. JAI provides structured cross-period/prior-return warnings, but no filing state or lock.
- [x] **D19 · Guided replacement boundary**: `Credit + replacement DRAFT` is available only for a direct Standard and for an Advance before a Final DRAFT exists. Receipt-only final Standards and issued Finals can be credited/refunded, but restarting uses a new Quote/manual flow.
- [x] **D20 · Whole-project cancellation**: a preview builds one correction per remaining formal charge document; confirmation atomically creates multiple Credit DRAFTs. The user reviews and issues each separately.
- [x] **D21 · One invoice workspace**: no new top-level module. The Invoices list gets kind filters/badges; Quotes expose billing-mode actions and a complete chain timeline; Credit creation starts only from a source document.
- [x] **D22 · Chain audit only**: add an append-only lifecycle/event trail for Quote/document-chain actions. M12 does not introduce a generic full-database audit system.
- [x] **D23 · Supply/prepayment date**: `supply_or_advance_date` is optional in a DRAFT and resolves to `invoice_date` on issue. Actual payment date/method/reference/note remain structured Payment data and may be listed on output.
- [x] **D24 · Issue party snapshot**: issue freezes seller/buyer legal identity, address, VAT/KVK fields, locale, immutable logo reference, `issued_at` and issuing user. Later master-data edits cannot alter the formal document identity.
- [x] **D25 · Exact artifacts**: every actual Download and every successful Send of an issued formal document or Refund Confirmation retains the exact PDF bytes, SHA-256, locale and filename. Preview/DRAFT does not retain. Equal hashes deduplicate; settlement changes may produce a new retained artifact; `EmailLog` links the bytes actually sent.
- [x] **D26 · Single currency**: M12 preserves the current one-currency-per-chain behaviour. Multi-currency/FX is not partially implemented.

## Domain Model and State Rules

### Document and quote enums

- `InvoiceDocumentKind = STANDARD | ADVANCE | FINAL | CREDIT_NOTE`.
- `QuoteSettlementMode = UNSET | DIRECT_INVOICE | RECEIPT_ONLY | FORMAL_ADVANCE`.
- `PaymentDirection = INCOMING | REFUND`.
- `InvoiceSettlementStatus = OPEN | PARTIALLY_SETTLED | SETTLED | REFUND_DUE`.
- `InvoiceCreditStatus = NOT_CREDITED | PARTIALLY_CREDITED | CREDITED`.
- Existing `InvoicePaidStatus` adds `NOT_APPLICABLE`; Credit Notes use it because ordinary incoming payments cannot be posted to them.
- Request-only enums include `AdvanceInputMode = GROSS_AMOUNT | PERCENTAGE` and `CreditLineInputMode = QUANTITY | GROSS_AMOUNT`.

### Quote-mode transition matrix

| Current mode | First relevant action | Locked mode | Allowed continuation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `UNSET` | Existing Quote→Invoice conversion | `DIRECT_INVOICE` | One direct Standard chain, payments and eligible replacement |
| `UNSET` | Existing Quote payment creation | `RECEIPT_ONLY` | M11.5 deposits→one complete Standard invoice |
| `UNSET` | Advance DRAFT creation | `FORMAL_ADVANCE` | Advance(s)→Final |
| Any locked mode | An action from another row | unchanged | `409 MODE_CONFLICT` |

Mode selection and its first action commit in one Quote-locked transaction. A failed action leaves `UNSET`. A formal chain requires at least one issued Advance before Final creation.

### Settlement equations

All amounts below have transaction-currency and `base_*` counterparts and are recomputed by backend services from locked snapshots:

```text
net_charge = payable_before_payments - issued_credit_total
net_cash = incoming_payment_total - refund_total
due_amount = max(net_charge - net_cash, 0)
refund_due_amount = max(net_cash - net_charge, 0)
```

- `payable_before_payments` is the charge represented by that document: full Standard/Advance charge, or the Final residual after frozen Advance applications.
- Document-chain totals sum independent charge documents and corrections exactly once. M11.5 receipt-only deposits remain cash against its one Standard invoice, not separate formal charges.
- Credit entitlement is allocated in Credit Note issue order, then ID order. A refund cannot exceed both its Credit Note's remaining refundable entitlement and the chain's `refund_due_amount`.
- Lifecycle is historical, not a substitute for settlement. An issued document can remain `SENT` while becoming `CREDITED`; `COMPLETED` does not erase credit/refund state.
- For a charge document, settlement is `REFUND_DUE` when `refund_due_amount>0`, `SETTLED` when both due values are zero, `PARTIALLY_SETTLED` when positive incoming cash covers only part of net charge, otherwise `OPEN`. A Credit Note has `paid_status=NOT_APPLICABLE` and zero ordinary due; it is `REFUND_DUE` while its allocated refundable entitlement remains, otherwise `SETTLED`. `refunded_amount` distinguishes partial from full refund without inventing another lifecycle.

### Formal Advance allocation

1. The Quote must be `ACCEPTED`, unconverted, `NL_DOMESTIC`, single-currency and mode `UNSET` or `FORMAL_ADVANCE`.
2. Gross input is rounded to the currency minor unit. Percentage input is validated as positive, applied to the original accepted Quote gross, then rounded once to the minor unit.
3. Aggregate the Quote's persisted line/document tax snapshots into stable VAT buckets.
4. Subtract the then-remaining net issued Advance coverage; pre-Final Advance credits reopen capacity.
5. Allocate the requested gross proportionally over remaining taxable/VAT components with the M11.5 integer-minor-unit largest-remainder algorithm.
6. No component is negative, taxable+VAT=gross for every bucket, and cumulative net Advances cannot exceed the accepted Quote snapshots.
7. `calculate` is side-effect free. Creation persists the same authoritative result; issue revalidates it under Quote/document locks.

### Final applications and editing

1. Final creation locks the Quote, requires no open Advance DRAFT, at least one issued Advance and no existing Final, then snapshots the accepted Quote as the initial full-project content.
2. For every issued Advance, calculate `issued charge − pre-Final issued credits`; omit zero remainder. Apply remaining Advances by issue date, document number and ID.
3. Persist document-level and per-VAT-bucket application snapshots. Final `payable_before_payments = full Final gross − application gross`.
4. Unpaid Advance balances remain due on those Advances; only the residual Final charge accepts Final payments. Project due is derived across the chain.
5. A Final DRAFT edit reruns pricing in services, reallocates its editable full-project tax buckets against the frozen applications and rejects a negative residual in any bucket. Quote, Customer, currency and treatment never change.
6. The response exposes original Quote totals, edited Final full-project totals, variance and applied Advance details. Issue freezes the result; later Advance credits never rewrite the application rows.

### Credit basis and issue

1. Issue of every charge document creates immutable `invoice_credit_basis_line` rows from its actual charge after applications: Standard/Advance use their charge snapshots; Final uses only its residual charge basis.
2. A Credit DRAFT references exactly one issued non-Credit source. Drafts do not reserve coverage; calculate/create use current remaining basis, and issue locks/revalidates it. A stale/competing draft receives `409`.
3. Full coverage consumes every remaining basis component. Partial quantity cannot exceed remaining source quantity. Partial gross cannot exceed that line's remaining gross and is deterministically split across its remaining net/VAT components.
4. Description, quantity/unit, VAT treatment/rate/effect, currency/base amounts and source references are inherited snapshots, not freely selected tax input.
5. Credit date cannot precede source invoice date. `supply_or_advance_date` follows the unified default rule.
6. At Credit issue, persist `affects_revenue`: true for Standard/Final; for Advance, true only if the chain already has an issued Final.
7. Issued credit coverage never exceeds source basis in quantity, gross, taxable, VAT or base amounts, including concurrent issues.

### Replacement, compensation and cancellation

- A guided replacement begins from an issued Credit Note that fully/partially corrects an eligible direct Standard or pre-Final Advance. It creates one linked DRAFT from the credited basis; the DRAFT is editable under the same source/chain invariants and gets a new number only on issue.
- A compensating invoice begins from an erroneous issued Credit Note and mirrors the Credit's charge/tax basis. It is an Advance only when the corrected source was an Advance and the Quote is still safely pre-Final; otherwise it is a supplemental Standard. This is a new positive tax/revenue event, not deletion of the Credit.
- Whole formal-project cancellation previews every issued charge document in the formal chain with remaining credit basis: Advance, Final and any linked supplemental Standard. Confirmation locks the complete chain and atomically creates one full-remaining Credit DRAFT per source; no number/tax/report event occurs until each draft is reviewed and issued.

### Payment and refund mutation

- `INCOMING` keeps the existing positive-amount rules and links to a charge Invoice or, only in M11.5, a Quote. `REFUND` is positive, links to one issued Credit Note and never has `payment_tax`.
- Refund create/edit/delete locks Quote (when present), sorted charge sources, issued Credits, all incoming/refund rows, Final applications and aggregate rows in the documented global order.
- Every mutation recomputes the affected document and chain. It rejects overpayment under existing branches, over-refund, refund-before-credit, cross-company links and any edit/delete that would leave existing refunds without issued Credit coverage.
- Refund Confirmation displays refund date, method, reference, note, Credit Note/source references, amount and post-refund remaining entitlement. It is not a Credit Note or VAT invoice.

### Settings and errors

- Credit-number settings mirror existing invoice-number settings: typed prefix/padding/next-start controls, independent sequence state and preview; existing invoice settings continue to cover Standard/Advance/Final.
- `404`: missing or cross-company resources without identity leakage.
- `409`: mode conflict, stale calculation/basis, competing draft/Final, lifecycle conflict or serialization/concurrency retry exhaustion.
- `422`: invalid amount/percentage/quantity/date, unsupported VAT treatment, uncovered VAT bucket, over-credit, over-refund or invalid relationship.
- Error bodies use stable machine-readable codes plus localized-display-safe detail; the frontend branches on codes, never English prose.

## Data Model and Additive Migration

### Modified existing tables

- **`quote`**: add non-null `settlement_mode` (default/backfill below) and nullable `settlement_mode_locked_at`.
- **`invoice`**: add non-null `document_kind`; nullable indexed Quote provenance; `supply_or_advance_date`; `issued_at`/`issued_by_user_id`; authoritative payable/credited/refunded/due/refund-due aggregates and settlement/credit status caches with base-currency parity. Retain the existing invoice-number storage for every formal document; sequence selection depends on kind.
- **`payment`**: add non-null `direction`; nullable indexed `credit_note_id`. DB checks enforce: incoming rows use Invoice/Quote context and no Credit; refund rows use exactly one Credit and no Quote-origin tax allocation.
- Keep `quote.converted_invoice_id` for backward compatibility; `document-chain` relations become authoritative for new flows.

### New normalized tables

- **`invoice_party_snapshot`**: one-to-one issue snapshot of seller/buyer names, legal/trade identity, structured addresses, VAT/KVK/contact fields, locale, immutable logo reference and provenance quality (`NATIVE_ISSUE | MIGRATED_CURRENT_STATE`).
- **`final_advance_application`**: Final↔Advance relation, stable order and transaction/base taxable, VAT and gross application totals.
- **`final_advance_application_tax`**: one row per applied source/target VAT bucket with rate/treatment/effect/ICP snapshots and transaction/base amounts.
- **`invoice_credit_basis_line`**: immutable source charge basis by issued document line, including charge quantity, net/VAT/gross, base amounts and tax snapshot identity.
- **`invoice_correction`**: one Credit Note→one source Invoice, issue-time totals and frozen `affects_revenue`.
- **`invoice_correction_line`**: selected basis line, mode/input provenance and credited quantity/net/VAT/gross/base snapshots.
- **`invoice_relation`**: typed positive-document relations for `REPLACEMENT_OF` and `COMPENSATES_CREDIT`; correction and Final application semantics stay in their dedicated normalized tables.
- **`document_chain_event`**: append-only company/Quote/Invoice actor, event type, timestamp and structured safe metadata for mode lock, create/issue/cancel/delete, application, credit, replacement, compensation, payment and refund lifecycle events.
- **`document_artifact`**: owning Invoice or refund Payment, artifact kind, exact PDF bytes, SHA-256, locale, filename, creation reason (`DOWNLOAD | SEND`), creation time and renderer version; unique owner+kind+hash deduplicates exact bytes. `email_log` gains nullable artifact FK.
- Credit-number sequence/settings use the existing typed settings/numbering architecture rather than a `max+1` column.

Every new business root/child follows current `company_id`/RLS ownership patterns where required. All monetary columns are `NUMERIC`/`Decimal`; descriptions are `text`; FKs and ORM relationships own cascade behaviour. Tax/application/correction rows remain normalized rather than adding nullable tax FKs to a wide table.

### Legacy backfill and compatibility

1. All existing Invoices become `STANDARD`; `payable_before_payments` starts from the persisted total. Do not recalculate prices, tax, payments, statuses or report history.
2. A Quote with any quote-origin M11.5 Payment becomes `RECEIPT_ONLY`; an already-converted Quote without quote-origin Payments becomes `DIRECT_INVOICE`; every other Quote becomes `UNSET`.
3. Backfill Invoice Quote provenance from `quote.converted_invoice_id`; leave the compatibility backlink intact.
4. Every existing Payment becomes `INCOMING`.
5. Existing issued Invoices receive full Standard credit-basis rows from persisted line/tax snapshots, never current VAT dictionaries.
6. Existing issued Invoice party snapshots use migration-time current Company/Customer data marked `MIGRATED_CURRENT_STATE`; do not invent historical `issued_at`/actor values. New issues use `NATIVE_ISSUE`.
7. The event log begins with M12 actions; migration may record an explicit migration marker but never fabricates historical user events.
8. Use multiple reviewable additive migrations following the current head. No table/column drop or migration baseline collapse. Upgrade/downgrade/head tests cover PostgreSQL constraints, RLS and production-shaped legacy rows.

## Reporting and Audit Semantics

### BTW and ICP

- Standard charge behaviour remains invoice-date based. Formal Advance issue adds its tax event on the Advance invoice date. Final issue adds only its residual tax after frozen applications; it does not reread Advance payments.
- Receipt-only M11.5 continues its existing payment-date `payment_tax` plus final full-invoice offset path. Formal and receipt-only sources are mutually exclusive by Quote mode.
- An issued Credit Note emits the exact negative source tax snapshots on the Credit date. Cross-border source treatment and `requires_icp` are inherited so ICP receives the matching negative correction.
- Refund cash has no VAT event. Payment mutation never changes formal Advance/Final/Credit tax snapshots.
- Reports expose structured warning records containing source/Credit numbers, event dates/periods, amount and a correction-guidance code when the correction is in a different/prior period. They never claim a return was or was not filed.

### P/L and Dashboard

- Standard issue recognises its existing net revenue. Advance issue recognises no revenue. Final issue recognises its full edited project net, not merely the residual payable.
- Standard/Final Credit Notes subtract their correction net on Credit date. An Advance Credit subtracts revenue only when its persisted `affects_revenue=true`; pre-Final Advance credits have no P/L effect because the later Final re-establishes the full then-current project basis.
- A complete formal-project cancellation after Final therefore reaches zero by crediting each Advance charge, the residual Final charge and any linked supplemental Standard separately, without changing application history.
- Dashboard totals use the same reporting services/events; they do not create a second calculation path.

### Append-only lifecycle audit

- Events are written in the same transaction as the action and contain IDs/codes and non-secret snapshots only. User-entered HTML, SMTP credentials and raw PDF bytes never enter event metadata.
- No update/delete API exists for events. RLS restricts reads. DB/ORM cascades may remove draft-chain events only if their owning not-yet-issued root is legitimately deleted; issued-document event history is retained under existing record-retention policy.

## Issue Snapshots, PDF, Email and Artifact Retention

- DRAFT preview uses current draft data and creates no artifact. Issue first validates/defaults `supply_or_advance_date`, allocates the correct number, freezes party/credit basis/tax metadata and commits atomically.
- Every issued PDF identifies its kind prominently and includes required number/date/supply-or-advance date, frozen seller/buyer details and source/chain references. Final lists every frozen Advance application; Credit lists its source and corrected lines; Standard/Advance/Final settlement sections list structured payments/refunds as relevant.
- Download renders bytes, stores/deduplicates the exact bytes, then returns those bytes. Successful Send stores/deduplicates first and commits `EmailLog.artifact_id` for the actual attachment; a failed send records failure under existing rules but no successful-send artifact relation.
- A later Download after payment/refund state changes may render a different hash and is retained separately. Repeated identical renders reuse the existing artifact. Historical artifacts are immutable and downloadable even after master data or settlement changes.
- Refund Confirmation has its own labels/template/default EN/ZH email settings and artifact owner. It explicitly references the Credit/source but is neither a VAT invoice nor a replacement for the Credit Note.
- Existing escaping, `nh3`, SVG/logo, SSRF, RFC 6266 filename and multipage-footer protections remain regression gates. Jinja/Vue perform presentation only, never authoritative money allocation.

## Numbering, Transactions and Concurrency

- Standard/Advance/Final allocate from the existing invoice sequence only on `DRAFT→SENT/COMPLETED`. Credit Notes allocate from the separate Credit sequence at the same lifecycle point. DRAFT preview never consumes a number.
- Both series support configured starts, prefixes/padding and gap skipping through the existing typed sequence service, DB uniqueness and retry path; never use `max+1`.
- Global lock order: **Quote → sorted charge/source Invoices → sorted Credit Notes → Payments/Refunds → Final applications/correction rows → numbering state**. Services that need a suffix only acquire the applicable prefix in this order.
- Mode lock + first action, Advance issue, Final create/issue, Credit issue, replacement/compensation creation, project-cancellation draft creation, payment/refund mutation and numbering each use one outer transaction with no intermediate commit.
- Required race outcomes: two Advance creates leave one DRAFT; cumulative Advance issues cannot exceed capacity; two Final creates leave one; concurrent Credits cannot over-credit; payment/refund edits cannot invalidate coverage; invoice and Credit numbers remain unique; a losing stale command returns `409` with no partial rows/events/artifacts.
